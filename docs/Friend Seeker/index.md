
# **Friend Seeker** - Find People Like You

**Overview**

Friend Seeker is a smart web app that helps you connect with people who share your interests. Powered by machine learning, it analyzes a quick survey and matches you with the group that best fits your lifestyle. Instantly, you’ll see your group’s profile, interactive charts, and even explore other communities to compare.

**Key features**

- Personalized Matching – Find your place by answering just a few simple questions.
- Data-Driven Insights – Discover your group’s unique characteristics with clear, interactive visuals.
- Explore & Compare – Check out other groups to see how they differ.
- Fun & Engaging – From age and education to pets and favorite places, see what makes each community unique.

**Built With**

- Python – the brain of the app
- Streamlit – sleek and simple web interface
- PyCaret – machine learning for smart clustering
- Plotly Express – beautiful, interactive charts
- Pandas – efficient data processing
